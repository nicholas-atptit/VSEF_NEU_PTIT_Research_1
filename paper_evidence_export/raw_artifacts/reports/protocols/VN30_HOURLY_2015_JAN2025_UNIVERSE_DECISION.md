# VN30 Hourly 2015 January 2025 Universe Decision

## Decision

Use the official VN30 January 2025 review universe as the benchmark universe for the VN30 hourly 2015 data-readiness track.

The evaluation window starts in 2025, so the benchmark universe must be frozen at or near the start of the evaluation period. Later or current constituents would introduce look-ahead universe bias because they reflect information unavailable at the evaluation start.

VN30 January 2025 effective period: 03/02/2025 to 01/08/2025.
